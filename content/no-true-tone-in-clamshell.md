---
title: Macbook True Tone does not work in clamshell mode
publish: "true"
---

I (finally) started using my macbook in clamshell mode with this Acer CB272K 4K monitor with Type C Power Delivery.
It did not occur to me that the true tone was not working until.. On my 2nd day of using this monitor, when I opened my macbook for accessing my passkey (with fingerprint scanner), I noticed that my screen had become a little yellow(ish).
I checked my display settings, and there was no option for true tone for the external monitor. A quick google search that led to [this reddit thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/MacOS/comments/m3xm2d/close_lid_and_true_tone_is_off/) (of course) revealed that the true tone sensors are in the macbook camera, and they won't work when the lid is closed.
